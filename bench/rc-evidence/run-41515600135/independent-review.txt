Hardware and comparison limits
Each runner exposes four logical CPUs. Memtx modes report AMD EPYC 7763; Vinyl
with native disabled reports Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, while Vinyl enabled
reports AMD EPYC 9V74. Modes use separate runners and are not matched engine or
native comparisons. Ordinary and profiled phases are sequential, so differences
are descriptive and confounded by time/JIT/heap drift, not a causal estimate of
profiler overhead. The small, single-host, closed-loop, same-shard workload does
not establish disk capacity, physical scale-out, overload behavior or candidate
improvements. Historical checks-enabled paired studies remain instrumented-run
evidence and cannot be numerically converted into production performance.
